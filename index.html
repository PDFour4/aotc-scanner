What to do

Everything stays on this device. There is no server and no account. Your figures are computed in this browser and saved only to this browser's local storage. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — which is deliberate: IRC §7216 makes it a criminal offence to use taxpayer information for any purpose other than preparing the return, and the surest way never to do that is never to hold it.

Every conclusion cites the statute behind it so it can be checked against the primary source. This is a research tool, not tax advice. Have a preparer review anything before it is filed.
